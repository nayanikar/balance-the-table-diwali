Who's holding the table?

A stick-figure guide for the shared potluck plate. Every guest who's coming becomes a tiny figure holding it up. The more lopsided the table gets, the more the figures under the heavy end struggle.

1 · Live demo

Play with the weight

Every figure picks its own pose from how much room it has under the plate. Pile dishes on one side and watch the corner figure end up carrying it on its back.

2 · The pose ladder

Seven poses, picked by headroom

h = the gap in px between the floor under a figure and the underside of the plate directly above it. Look up which band h falls in and that's the pose. Inside a band, bend the knees or lean further so the hands (or back) touch the plate exactly.

3 · Personality

Quirks when they're relaxed

Only in the relaxed pose (one palm up) is the free hand free. Pick its quirk from a hash of the guest's id, so each guest keeps theirs. Once the plate gets heavy, everyone switches to two hands. The quirk comes back when the table rebalances.

5 · Rules

Do / don't

  • Feet always on the floor (or on a crate). There's a visible floor line under the plate. Don't let figures float in mid-air.
  • Heads never go through the plate. Every pose keeps the head top under the plate's underside. Don't just push figures up until their hands touch.
  • Hands (or the back) touch the plate exactly at the underside above the figure. Draw figures behind the plate so contact points tuck under the rim.
  • Every figure reacts to the tilt every frame. They're re-posed as the plate swings, so the heavy end visibly sinks onto them. Don't use one fixed pose for everyone.
  • One figure per guest who's coming (the host included, in a party hat). Spread them under the middle 75% of the plate, up to 10, then add “+N helping” on the floor.
  • Face the centre: figures left of centre face right, figures right of centre face left. Bent-over figures lean toward the middle.
  • The new guest's figure pops in (scale 0→1 with overshoot, 400 ms) at the end nearest their side when they pick, painted gold. Its quirk is wave.
  • Strain is shown, never said: sweat drops, a slight shake and effort marks. No text.
  • Reduced motion: no shake, no wave wiggle, no sparkler spin, no pop. Poses still change.
